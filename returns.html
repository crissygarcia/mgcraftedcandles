Returns & Exchange Policy

Last updated: 08 November 2026

We want you to love your MG Crafted Creations order.

Handmade variations

Our products are handmade, personalized, or made to order. Small variations in colour, finish, shape, and fragrance are part of their handmade nature and are not considered faults.

Damaged, defective, or incorrect items

Returns or exchanges may be accepted for damaged, defective, or incorrect items. Please contact us within 48 hours of delivery at orders@mgcraftedcreations.com or via WhatsApp at +971 52 776 0547, with your order details and clear photos of the item and packaging.

Custom orders

Personalized, custom-made, sale, and event-order items cannot usually be returned or exchanged unless they arrive damaged, defective, or different from the confirmed order details.

Approved returns

Where a return is approved, we will offer a replacement, store credit, or refund as appropriate. Return delivery arrangements will be confirmed before anything is sent back.
