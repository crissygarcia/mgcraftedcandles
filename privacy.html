Privacy Policy

Last updated: 08 November 2026

MG Crafted Creations respects your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you visit our website or contact us.

Information we collect

We may collect your name, phone number, email address, delivery details, and order or event information when you contact us, place an order, or submit an enquiry.

How we use your information

We use this information to respond to enquiries, process orders, arrange delivery or collection, personalize products and event orders, contact you about your order, and improve our products and website.

Sharing and payment information

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We only share it when necessary to process your order, such as with delivery or payment providers. Payment details are handled by our selected payment provider. MG Crafted Creations does not store card numbers or CVV details.

Contact us

We take reasonable measures to protect your information. To ask about the personal information we hold about you, email orders@mgcraftedcreations.com.
