Delivery Policy

Last updated: 08 November 2026

MG Crafted Creations currently delivers within the UAE.

Delivery timing and costs

Delivery costs, available locations, and estimated delivery times are shown at checkout or confirmed before your order is accepted. Timing may vary depending on product availability, personalization, order quantity, and delivery location.

Ready-made items are prepared for dispatch within the time confirmed with you. Personalized, made-to-order, and event orders may require additional preparation time. We will confirm the estimated completion and delivery date before beginning these orders.

Your delivery details

Please ensure that your delivery address and phone number are correct. We are not responsible for delays caused by incorrect information, unavailable recipients, or circumstances outside our control.

For a time-sensitive event order, please contact us before placing an order at orders@mgcraftedcreations.com.
