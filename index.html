HELP

Before you order

Can I choose my fragrance?

Yes. Ready-made candles have their listed fragrance, while custom orders can be created around your preferred scent profile.

Can I personalize the candle?

Yes. Names, dates, short messages and event details can be added depending on the candle and label format.

Do you accept bulk orders?

Yes. Weddings, baby showers, birthdays, corporate giveaways and other events are welcome. Bulk pricing is quoted based on quantity and customization.

How long do custom orders take?

Lead time depends on quantity, customization and event date. For event orders, we recommend contacting us as early as possible.
